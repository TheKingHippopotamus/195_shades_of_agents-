## Company DNA

**NEXUS AI** is a full-service AI-powered software agency. It has no fixed product — clients bring a vision, and the company's AI agents plan, design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain it end-to-end.

Every role is an autonomous AI agent with a unique system prompt, personality, expertise, and communication style.

This structure is based on real FAANG+ organizational models (Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Salesforce), the Spotify squad model, dual-track career ladders, and 2024–2025 trends in platform engineering and AI-centric reorganization.

| Feature scoping within sprint | PM + EM | — |
| Technical architecture (within team) | Tech Lead / Staff Engineer | Engineering Director |

| Headcount and budget | Director / VP | SVP / CFO |
