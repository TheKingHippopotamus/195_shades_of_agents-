## 3. PHASE 2: QUALIFY & DISCOVER (Sales + Pre-Sales)

### 3.1 Discovery Meeting Structure

```
MEETING 1: EXECUTIVE DISCOVERY (60 min)
  Attendees: AE (117/118) + Client exec(s)
  
  Agenda:
  00-05: Introductions, NEXUS AI 2-min overview
  05-25: Client's world (open-ended questions):
         "Tell me about your business..."
         "What's your biggest technology challenge right now?"
         "What happens if you don't solve this in the next 12 months?"
  25-40: Specific needs (guided questions):
         "What have you tried so far?"
         "What does success look like?"
         "What's your timeline and budget range?"
  40-55: NEXUS AI relevant capabilities + case study
  55-60: Next steps — schedule technical discovery

MEETING 2: TECHNICAL DISCOVERY (90 min)
  Attendees: SALES-ENG (122) + SOL-ARCH (123) + Client technical team
  Activate: DISC-AGENT (187) to structure findings
  
  Deep-dive into: Architecture, data, integrations, security,
  compliance, technical constraints, team capabilities
  (Follow Discovery Questionnaire — Template 1 in Templates Pack)

MEETING 3: SOLUTION PRESENTATION (60 min)
  Attendees: AE + SALES-ENG + SOL-ARCH + Client decision makers
  
  Present: Proposed approach, architecture overview, team composition,
  timeline, pricing model, relevant case study with results
  
  Key principle: Present THEIR story, not our capabilities.
  "Based on what we learned, here's how we'd solve YOUR problem..."
```

### 3.2 MEDDIC Qualification

```
FOR EACH OPPORTUNITY, AE MUST DOCUMENT:

M — METRICS
  "What business metric will this improve?"
  "By how much? How do you measure it today?"
  Goal: Quantified success criteria we can reference post-delivery.

E — ECONOMIC BUYER
  "Who has budget authority for this investment?"
  "Have they approved similar investments before?"
  Goal: Know exactly who signs and what they care about.

D — DECISION CRITERIA
  "What criteria will you use to evaluate vendors?"
  "What's most important: speed, quality, cost, expertise?"
  Goal: Align our proposal to their criteria.

D — DECISION PROCESS
  "Walk me through your approval process."
  "Who else needs to be involved?"
  "Is there a procurement/legal review?"
  Goal: Map every step between "yes" and signed contract.

I — IDENTIFY PAIN
  "What's the biggest pain point this addresses?"
  "What's the cost of inaction?"
  Goal: Understand emotional + financial pain. Urgency driver.

C — CHAMPION
  "Who internally is advocating for this project?"
  "How can we help them build the internal case?"
  Goal: Arm our champion with the ammunition to sell internally.

MEDDIC SCORE (0-6): One point per element documented.
  6/6: Well-qualified, strong chance of closing
  4-5: Qualified, some gaps to fill
  <4: Under-qualified, needs more discovery
```

## 4. PHASE 3: CLOSE (Sales + Legal + Finance)

### 4.1 Proposal Structure

```
PROPOSAL DOCUMENT:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 page)
   - Client's challenge (in their words)
   - Our approach (high-level)
   - Expected outcomes (tied to their metrics)
   - Investment summary

2. UNDERSTANDING YOUR NEEDS (2-3 pages)
   - Summary of discovery findings
   - Key requirements
   - Success criteria

3. PROPOSED SOLUTION (3-5 pages)
   - Solution architecture (visual)
   - Technology approach
   - Team composition
   - Methodology (agile delivery)
   - Timeline with milestones

4. INVESTMENT (1-2 pages)
   - Pricing model (T&M / Fixed / Hybrid)
   - Phase breakdown with costs
   - Payment schedule
   - What's included / excluded

5. WHY NEXUS AI (1-2 pages)
   - Relevant case study with measurable results
   - Team credentials
   - Differentiators

6. NEXT STEPS
   - Contract timeline
   - Kickoff plan
   - Key contacts

PRICING MODELS:
  Time & Materials: Best for unclear scope, ongoing work
    Rate: Per agent per month (transparent)
    Billing: Monthly actuals
    Cap: Optional monthly/total cap

  Fixed Price: Best for well-defined scope
    Price: Based on EST-AGENT (188) estimate + risk margin
    Milestones: Payment tied to deliverables
    Change orders: Formal CR process for scope changes

  Hybrid: Fixed base + T&M for additional scope
    Common: Fixed for core MVP + T&M for enhancements

MARGIN TARGETS:
  Project gross margin target: >50%
  Minimum acceptable margin: 35%
  Below 35%: CFO (005) + CEO (001) approval required
```

### 4.2 Contract Process

```
CONTRACT FLOW:
  Day 1: CONTRACTS (156) drafts MSA + SOW from templates
  Day 2: Internal review (VP-LEGAL, CFO for >$500K)
  Day 3: Send to client
  Day 4-10: Client legal review + redlines
  Day 10-14: Negotiation (CONTRACTS + AE)
  Day 14-17: Final approval + signature

KEY CONTRACT TERMS:
  IP: NEXUS AI retains IP of reusable components; client owns custom work
  Liability: Capped at 12 months of fees
  Termination: 30 days notice (either party)
  Data: Data processing agreement (DPA) for GDPR compliance
  SLA: Defined in SOW per project
  Warranty: 30-90 days post-delivery (bug fixes)
  Change Orders: Formal process, signed before work begins

APPROVAL MATRIX:
  <$100K:   DIR-SALES + VP-LEGAL sign
  $100K-$500K: VP-SALES + VP-LEGAL + CFO sign
  >$500K:   CRO + CLO + CFO + CEO sign
```

## 5. PHASE 4: DELIVER (Engineering + Product + Design)

### 5.1 Kickoff Protocol

```
INTERNAL KICKOFF (Before client kickoff):
  Attendees: All assigned agents
  Duration: 2 hours
  
  Agenda:
  1. Client context: AE presents discovery findings (20 min)
  2. Solution overview: SOL-ARCH presents architecture (20 min)
  3. Project plan: TPM presents timeline + milestones (15 min)
  4. Team roles: Each agent confirms their role (10 min)
  5. Risks: Known risks + mitigation (15 min)
  6. Ways of working: Communication, ceremonies, tools (15 min)
  7. Q&A (15 min)
  8. First sprint planning prep (10 min)

CLIENT KICKOFF:
  Attendees: NEXUS AI team leads + client team
  Duration: 90 min
  
  Agenda:
  1. Introductions + team structure (15 min)
  2. Project goals + success criteria recap (15 min)
  3. Solution approach overview (20 min)
  4. Ways of working: tools, cadence, communication (15 min)
  5. Timeline + milestones (10 min)
  6. Q&A (15 min)
  
  Output: Kickoff recording, shared project workspace, first milestone date
```

### 5.2 Client Communication Cadence

```
DURING DELIVERY:
  Daily:     Async updates in shared Slack channel (engineering team)
  Weekly:    Status meeting with client PM (TPM leads)
  Bi-weekly: Sprint demo (PM presents, engineers demo features)
  Monthly:   Executive status report (SR-TPM + PM)
  Ad-hoc:    Escalation meetings as needed

STATUS UPDATE FORMAT (Weekly):
  - What was delivered this week
  - What's planned for next week
  - Risks / blockers / decisions needed
  - Burndown / progress toward milestone
  - Client action items (if any)

COMMUNICATION RULES:
  1. No surprises — client should never be surprised by bad news
  2. Proactive — share risks when they emerge, not when they materialize
  3. Solutions — always present problems with proposed solutions
  4. Single voice — PM is primary client contact for project matters
  5. Escalation — CSM for relationship, TPM for delivery, AE for commercial
```

### 5.3 Change Management

```
SCOPE CHANGES DURING DELIVERY:
  1. Client or team identifies potential scope change
  2. PM assesses impact (effort, timeline, cost)
  3. PM presents Change Request to client:
     - What's changing
     - Impact on timeline
     - Impact on budget
     - Recommended approach
  4. Client approves/rejects CR
  5. If approved: SOW amendment signed (if >10% budget impact)
  6. Backlog updated, sprint plan adjusted

CHANGE REQUEST TEMPLATE:
  CR-{NNN}: {Description}
  Requested by: {Client/Internal}
  Impact: Timeline: +{X} days | Budget: +${X}K
  Justification: {Why this change is needed}
  Options: A) Include in scope B) Phase 2 C) Don't do
  Recommendation: Option {X}
  Approved by: {Client name} — Date: {X}
```

## 6. PHASE 5: GROW (Customer Success + Sales)

### 6.1 Post-Delivery Handoff

```
DELIVERY → CS HANDOFF (HANDOFF-AGENT 183 orchestrates):
  
  Day -14: HANDOFF-AGENT activates. Handoff checklist initiated.
  Day -7:  Knowledge transfer sessions (3-4 sessions)
  Day 0:   Official handoff meeting. All sign-offs complete.
  Day +1:  Warranty period begins. CSM takes primary relationship.
  Day +14: Warranty ends (standard). Engineering support drops off.
  Day +30: First health check by CSM. CSAT survey.
  Day +90: First QBR. Expansion discussion begins.

HANDOFF ATTENDEES:
  Outgoing: PM, Engineering Lead, TPM
  Incoming: SR-CSM (137), TAM (139), SUPPORT-T2 (141)
  Always: HANDOFF-AGENT (190)
```

### 6.2 Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

```
QBR STRUCTURE (60-90 min):
  
  SECTION 1: VALUE DELIVERED (20 min)
  Presenter: SR-CSM (137)
  Content:
  - Success metrics: baseline → current (with data)
  - Key milestones achieved
  - ROI calculation
  - User adoption metrics

  SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL HEALTH (15 min)
  Presenter: TAM (139)
  Content:
  - System performance metrics
  - Uptime / reliability report
  - Support ticket trends
  - Security / compliance status

  SECTION 3: ROADMAP & INNOVATION (20 min)
  Presenter: SR-CSM + AI-PM (071) or SR-PM (068)
  Content:
  - What's coming (product roadmap relevant to client)
  - Industry trends + AI opportunities
  - Recommended enhancements
  - New capabilities that could benefit client

  SECTION 4: STRATEGIC DISCUSSION (20 min)
  Led by: Client exec + SR-CSM
  Content:
  - Client's evolving business priorities
  - How NEXUS AI can support next phase
  - Expansion opportunities
  - Partnership deepening
  
  Output: QBR summary doc, action items, expansion proposal (if applicable)
```

### 6.3 Expansion & Renewal

```
EXPANSION SIGNALS (CSM monitors):
  ✅ Client mentions new project ideas
  ✅ Client asks about capabilities we haven't used
  ✅ Client's business is growing (headcount, revenue, new markets)
  ✅ Client satisfaction is high (NPS >8, CSAT >4.5)
  ✅ Usage is increasing beyond original scope
  ✅ Client refers us to other departments/contacts

EXPANSION PLAYBOOK:
  1. CSM identifies signal → Logs in CRM
  2. CSM + ACCT-MGR (124) discuss opportunity
  3. If qualified: Activate DISC-AGENT (187) for lightweight discovery
  4. EST-AGENT (188) provides estimate
  5. Proposal created (often lighter than new client — trust exists)
  6. Close expansion deal
  7. Activate delivery team (often overlaps with maintenance team)

RENEWAL PROCESS (Start 120 days before expiry):
  Day -120: CSM flags upcoming renewal. Health assessment.
  Day -90:  If health 🟢: Standard renewal. ACCT-MGR prepares terms.
            If health 🟡🔴: Save plan activated (VP-CS involved).
  Day -60:  Renewal proposal sent to client.
  Day -30:  Contract negotiation (if changes needed).
  Day -14:  Escalate unsigned renewals to VP-CS + CRO.
  Day 0:    Renewal signed (target) or extension period.

CHURN PREVENTION (At-Risk Protocol):
  Health 🟡 (At Risk):
  1. CSM creates save plan: identify root cause, propose solution
  2. Executive outreach: VP-CS or CRO calls client exec
  3. Engineering resources allocated for quick wins
  4. Weekly cadence until health improves
  
  Health 🔴 (Critical):
  1. All of the above PLUS:
  2. CEO or relevant C-suite executive reaches out
  3. On-site visit if possible
  4. Custom retention offer if needed (CFO approves)
  5. Daily internal updates until resolved
```
