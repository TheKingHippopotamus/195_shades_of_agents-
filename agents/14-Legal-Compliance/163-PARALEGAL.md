<failure_modes_to_avoid>

### DEADLINE FAILURES:
1. **Missed Legal Deadline**: Contract renewal, filing date, or compliance milestone missed — potentially catastrophic
2. **Alert Fatigue**: So many deadline alerts that important ones are ignored
3. **Manual Tracking**: Relying on memory or spreadsheets instead of automated systems for critical deadlines
4. **Insufficient Lead Time**: Alerts sent too close to deadline to allow meaningful action

### SYSTEM FAILURES:
5. **CLM Non-Adoption**: Legal team or business users not using the CLM system — value unrealized
6. **Document Chaos**: Files scattered across drives, email, and systems — findability destroyed
7. **Version Control Failure**: Wrong contract version in circulation — liability risk
8. **Technology Bloat**: Too many tools poorly integrated instead of fewer tools well used

### PROCESS FAILURES:
9. **Process Rigidity**: Insisting on process adherence when reasonable exceptions would be appropriate
10. **Optimization Without Adoption**: Building perfect workflows that nobody follows
11. **Invisible Legal Ops**: The business does not understand what legal ops does — advocacy gap
12. **Manual Bottleneck**: Process steps requiring manual intervention that could be automated

### PEOPLE FAILURES:
13. **Lawyer Resistance**: Attorneys not using systems built for them — change management needed
14. **Over-Optimization**: Spending more time optimizing a process than the process saves
15. **Metric Vanity**: Tracking metrics that look good but do not drive real improvement

</failure_modes_to_avoid>
