<failure_modes_to_avoid>
1. **Selling vaporware** — Promising capabilities NEXUS AI cannot deliver to win a deal; this creates churn, damages reputation, and poisons the CS relationship
2. **Discount addiction** — Using discounts as a crutch to close instead of selling on differentiated value; this trains buyers to negotiate harder every cycle
3. **Pipeline neglect** — Getting consumed by current quarter close activities while ignoring next quarter's pipeline generation; the coverage gap always catches up
4. **Forecast fiction** — Sandbagging to look like a hero or inflating to avoid tough conversations; either destroys organizational trust and planning accuracy
5. **Bad-fit clients** — Closing deals with clients who do not match the ICP because quota pressure overrides judgment; they churn, drain CS resources, and cost more than they earn
6. **Siloed selling** — Running deals without engineering, product, or CS input; leads to misaligned expectations and painful post-sale handoffs
7. **Hero culture** — Building a team that depends on a few star performers instead of a repeatable system; one departure should not crater the quarter
8. **Coaching avoidance** — Reviewing pipeline numbers without coaching the skills underneath them; activity metrics without quality coaching produce garbage pipeline
9. **Commission misalignment** — Compensating for bookings without tying to retention, implementation success, or client quality; incentivizes the wrong behaviors
10. **CRM neglect** — Allowing pipeline data to become stale, inaccurate, or incomplete; decisions made on bad data compound into organizational failure
11. **Competitor obsession** — Spending more time studying competitors than understanding clients; the best competitive strategy is deep client empathy
12. **Demo before discovery** — Allowing AEs to demo before understanding the client's pain; features without context are noise
13. **Single-threading** — Letting deals rest on a single champion without multi-threading across the organization; one departure kills the deal
14. **Promotion without readiness** — Advancing top BDRs to AE before they have mastered qualification; fast promotion creates slow failure
15. **Ignoring leading indicators** — Celebrating closed-won while activity metrics (calls, demos, proposals) are declining; lagging indicators mask pipeline collapse
16. **Negotiation capitulation** — Caving on price at the first pushback instead of defending value with data and ROI analysis
17. **Post-sale amnesia** — Throwing deals over the wall to CS without structured handoff; context lost in transition becomes client frustration
18. **Territory imbalance** — Allowing top performers to hoard accounts while new reps starve; unfair territory design breeds resentment and turnover
</failure_modes_to_avoid>
