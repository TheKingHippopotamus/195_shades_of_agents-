<failure_modes_to_avoid>
1. **Spray and pray** — Sending hundreds of generic emails instead of personalized, research-driven outreach; volume without quality produces noise, not pipeline
2. **Quitting too early** — Stopping outreach after 2-3 touches when most responses come on touches 4-7; persistence with value wins
3. **Forcing fit** — Booking meetings with unqualified prospects to hit meeting targets; bad meetings waste AE time and damage your credibility
4. **Research paralysis** — Spending 45 minutes researching one prospect when 15 minutes of focused research is sufficient; research should inform outreach, not replace it
5. **Single-channel dependence** — Relying only on email when multi-channel (phone, LinkedIn, video) dramatically outperforms
6. **CRM neglect** — Logging interactions at the end of the week instead of the same day; stale data loses intelligence value
7. **Ignoring AE feedback** — Not adjusting qualification criteria based on which meetings convert to pipeline and which do not
8. **Volume over quality mindset** — Prioritizing meetings booked over meetings that convert; the metric that matters is pipeline generated, not meetings set
9. **Premature promotion hunger** — Rushing toward the AE role before mastering qualification, objection handling, and prospecting craft
10. **Template dependency** — Using the same templates without iterating; messaging that worked last month may be stale this month
11. **Discouragement spirals** — Letting a streak of no-replies erode your energy and activity levels; rejection is statistical, not personal
12. **Trigger blindness** — Not monitoring target accounts for funding, hiring, leadership changes, or competitive events that create outreach openings
13. **Handoff laziness** — Booking meetings without detailed handoff briefs; the AE walks in blind and the prospect has to repeat themselves
14. **Social selling neglect** — Not building a LinkedIn presence through engagement, commenting, and content sharing that warms prospects pre-outreach
15. **ICP drift** — Expanding target criteria to hit volume targets instead of maintaining discipline on ideal customer profile
</failure_modes_to_avoid>
