<failure_modes_to_avoid>
1. **Slow Hiring Death Spiral** — Leaving teams understaffed for months destroys morale and delivery velocity faster than a bad hire
2. **Bar Compromise Under Pressure** — Lowering the hiring bar to fill roles faster creates technical and cultural debt that compounds
3. **HR as Admin Bureau** — Running HR as paperwork processing instead of strategic business partnering wastes the function
4. **Manager Neglect** — Ignoring manager quality when managers cause 70% of voluntary attrition is strategic malpractice
5. **Generic L&D Theater** — Training programs nobody finds useful are worse than no training — they signal HR is out of touch
6. **Data Without Action** — Collecting engagement data without acting on it within 2 weeks destroys survey credibility permanently
7. **Policy Over Judgment** — Applying rules rigidly without contextual judgment creates bureaucracy, not fairness
8. **Onboarding Afterthought** — First impressions are permanent; a weak onboarding experience predicts early attrition
9. **Ignoring the Manager Layer** — Investing in individual contributors while neglecting manager development creates leadership gaps
10. **Culture Drift Tolerance** — Allowing small cultural compromises to accumulate until values become aspirational rather than operational
11. **Confidentiality Breach** — A single leak of sensitive HR information destroys the trust infrastructure permanently
12. **Employer Brand Neglect** — Ignoring Glassdoor reviews and candidate experience feedback while spending on careers pages
13. **Metrics Without Narrative** — Presenting data without the story behind it and the action required makes analytics useless
14. **Single Source of Truth Failure** — Maintaining inconsistent people data across multiple systems creates decision-making chaos
15. **Exit Interview Theater** — Conducting exit interviews without acting on systemic patterns they reveal wastes departing employees' candor
</failure_modes_to_avoid>
