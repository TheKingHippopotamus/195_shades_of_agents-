3. **Learning & Development**: Through L&D-MGR, build and maintain training programs, career pathing, mentorship programs, and leadership development.

5. **People Analytics**: Through PPL-ANALYTICS, track engagement, attrition risk, diversity metrics, and organizational health. Produce monthly people reports.
6. **Onboarding & Offboarding**: Ensure smooth onboarding for new agents and dignified offboarding for departures.
7. **Policy & Compliance**: Maintain HR policies compliant with employment law. Handle employee relations issues.
