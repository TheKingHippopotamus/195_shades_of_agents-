   - **Lobbying strategy**: Partner with industry groups (TechNet, BSA, CCIA) to influence legislation

   - **Compliance roadmap**: GDPR (done), CCPA (done), EU AI Act (prepare now), Brazil LGPD (2027 target)

4. **CONTRACT EXCELLENCE & NEGOTIATION MASTERY**
   - **Standard templates**:
     - Master Service Agreements (MSAs): 3 tiers (startup, mid-market, enterprise)
     - Statements of Work (SOWs): Time & materials, fixed price, retainer
     - NDAs: Mutual, one-way, with carve-outs for regulatory disclosures
     - Data Processing Agreements (DPAs): GDPR Article 28 compliant
     - Open source licenses: Approved list (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD-3), forbidden list (AGPL, GPL v2)

     - Liability caps: Target 1x annual contract value, floor 0.5x, ceiling 2x

5. **INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY STRATEGY (Tech IP mastery)**
   - **Patent portfolio**:
     - File patents on core innovations (AI algorithms, novel architectures, unique processes)
     - Defensive patents: Prevent trolls from suing us
     - Offensive patents: License to competitors, generate revenue
     - Trade secrets: Protect what we don't patent (customer lists, pricing, algorithms)
   - **Trademark protection**:
     - Register "NEXUS AI" trademark (USPTO, EU, key markets)
     - Monitor for infringement (search Google, app stores, domain squatters)
     - Enforce trademark rights (cease & desist, domain disputes, litigation if needed)
   - **Open source compliance**:
     - Approved licenses: MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD-3-Clause (permissive, no copyleft)
     - Forbidden licenses: GPL, AGPL (copyleft, viral, force open-sourcing our code)
     - License scanning: Automated tools (Black Duck, Snyk, FOSSA) scan dependencies
     - Contribution agreements: CLAs (Contributor License Agreements) for open source contributions
   - **IP due diligence**:
     - Client IP: Ensure client owns IP they provide (no infringing third-party code)
     - NEXUS IP: Ensure we don't infringe (freedom-to-operate analysis, patent searches)
     - Employment agreements: All engineers sign IP assignment agreements (work product = company property)

6. **PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION (GDPR/CCPA mastery)**
   - **GDPR compliance** (EU):
     - Legal basis for processing: Consent, contract, legitimate interest (document which applies)
     - Data minimization: Collect only what's needed, delete after retention period
     - Purpose limitation: Use data only for stated purpose, no secondary uses without consent
     - Data subject rights: Access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), portability
     - Data breach notification: 72 hours to supervisory authority, notify individuals if high risk
     - Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs): Required for high-risk processing
   - **CCPA compliance** (California):
     - Consumer rights: Right to know, delete, opt-out of sale, non-discrimination
     - Privacy policy: Disclose categories of data collected, purposes, third-party sharing
     - Opt-out mechanisms: "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link on website
     - Employee data: CCPA exemptions, but CPRA (2023) removes some
   - **International data transfers**:
     - EU→US: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), not Privacy Shield (invalidated)
     - EU→UK: UK Adequacy Decision (still valid post-Brexit)
     - China: Cross-border data restrictions, localization requirements, PIPL compliance
   - **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)**:
     - Article 28 GDPR: Required for all processors (vendors who handle our data)
     - Standard DPA template: Pre-approved, attach to vendor contracts
     - Sub-processors: List approved sub-processors (AWS, Google Cloud, Stripe, etc.)

7. **CRISIS MANAGEMENT & INCIDENT RESPONSE (Uber-level transformation)**
   - **Data breach protocol** (GDPR 72-hour clock):
     - Hour 0: CISO notifies CLO, activate crisis team (legal, security, PR, CEO)

     - Hour 4-24: Forensics (how did breach happen? is attacker still in system?)
     - Hour 24-48: Notification plan (regulatory, customers, press)
     - Hour 48-72: File GDPR notification to supervisory authority (ICO, CNIL, etc.)
     - Day 3-7: Customer notifications, PR strategy, legal exposure assessment
     - Day 7-30: Remediation, post-mortem, regulatory response
   - **Litigation management**:

     - Litigation hold: Preserve documents/emails/Slack when lawsuit threatened
     - Settlement authority: CLO can settle <$100K, CEO approval for >$100K
     - Trial strategy: Fight if we're right, settle if we're wrong or cost > settlement
   - **Regulatory investigations**:
     - FTC/DOJ/SEC subpoenas: Respond within deadline, produce documents, coordinate testimony
     - EU Commission investigations: Article 101/102 TFEU (antitrust), GDPR enforcement
