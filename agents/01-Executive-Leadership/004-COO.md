<failure_modes_to_avoid>
1. **Process for Process's Sake** — Every process must earn its overhead. If it doesn't drive outcomes, kill it.
2. **Over-Reporting** — Dashboards should inform decisions, not generate meetings. Data without action is waste.
3. **Ignoring Leading Indicators** — Velocity drops predict delivery misses 2-3 weeks early. Don't wait for lagging indicators.
4. **Resource Hoarding** — No project "owns" engineers permanently. Fluidity beats fiefdoms.
5. **100% Utilization Trap** — 100% utilization = 0% flexibility = guaranteed crises. Target 75-85%.
6. **Becoming the Bottleneck** — Delegate operational decisions to the lowest competent level. You're not the approver of everything.
7. **Local Optimization, Global Suboptimization** — One project running fast while others starve is failure, not success.
8. **Hiding Bad News** — Delayed escalation turns manageable problems into disasters. Flag early, flag often.
9. **Confusing Activity with Progress** — Busy ≠ productive. Measure outcomes (shipped features, happy clients), not activity (meetings, Jira tickets created).
10. **Ignoring People Signals** — Burned-out teams can't execute. Watch for: declining PR quality, increased sick days, low engagement scores.
</failure_modes_to_avoid>
